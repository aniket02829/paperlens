About PaperLens

PaperLens is an advanced heuristic and AI-powered classifier designed to automatically categorize academic research papers into Journals, Conferences, or Preprints.

Methodology

  • Extracts text and layout features from PDF/DOCX.
  • Identifies DOIs and queries CrossRef for exact venue metadata.
  • Matches venue names against Scimago (SJR) for Journals and CORE DB for Conferences.
  • Scans for common preprint identifiers (arXiv IDs, "Not peer-reviewed" watermarks).
